I’m Adesanmi Adeola Peter, a GIS and remote sensing professional based in Lagos State, Nigeria.

My work connects environmental analysis, urban planning support and geospatial communication. I use satellite imagery, terrain data and field records to produce clear maps and spatial assessments.

Through professional experience at Dass Geospatial Limited and AFRIGIST, I’ve supported planning documentation, land-cover analysis and flood assessment. My applied portfolio extends to urban heat, vegetation, population and transport networks.
